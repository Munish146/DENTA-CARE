Preventative Care

Daily Oral Wellness & Dental Tips

Expert recommendations from our Ramanathapuram dental specialists to keep your natural smile bright and healthy between clinical visits.

2-Minute Gentle Brushing

Use soft bristles at a 45-degree angle twice daily. Avoid aggressive scrubbing to safeguard your natural enamel.

Daily Interdental Flossing

Over 40% of tooth surfaces lie between teeth. Daily flossing prevents interproximal cavities and gum inflammation.

Enamel Acid Defense

Rinse with plain water after citrus fruits or coffee. Wait 30 minutes before brushing to allow enamel remineralization.

Bi-Annual Checkups

Routine 6-month checkups detect early micro-cavities before they cause discomfort, saving you time and cost.
