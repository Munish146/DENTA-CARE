Privacy & Protocols

Clinical Photography & Patient Privacy Standards

How we safely capture, store, and utilize clinical images strictly in compliance with healthcare data protection laws.

Do you require patient consent before taking treatment photos?

Yes, always. High-definition clinical photos are captured strictly for medical diagnosis and dental laboratory shade matching. They are never published without your written consent.

How securely are my 3D intraoral scans stored?

All digital scan files (STL/PLY) are encrypted using 256-bit AES encryption on HIPAA-compliant cloud servers, accessible exclusively by your attending clinician.

Can I request copies of my smile makeover photos?

Yes! We provide all patients with high-resolution digital copies of their pre-treatment and post-treatment results through our secure Patient Portal.
