Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our modern treatments, painless procedures, and easy scheduling.

Are dental treatments at DentaCare truly painless?

Yes! We use computerized precision local anesthesia that numbs tissues without needle sting, along with gentle micro-invasive lasers that preserve healthy teeth and eliminate discomfort.

How long does a full smile transformation usually take?

Simple cosmetic treatments like laser whitening or composite bonding take just 60 to 90 minutes. Porcelain veneers and clear aligners take between 1 to 6 weeks with comprehensive digital previews.

Do you provide zero-interest EMI or payment plans?

Absolutely. We partner with leading financial providers to offer 0% interest EMI options spanning 3, 6, and 12 months with no hidden surcharges or upfront fees.

Can I get same-day appointment for urgent tooth pain?

Yes. We maintain reserved daily emergency slots for trauma, acute pulpitis, or broken crowns. Call our emergency direct line at +91 98765 43210 for immediate triage.
