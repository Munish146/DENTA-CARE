Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before booking? Here is everything you need to know regarding your visit.

What happens if I need to reschedule or cancel my appointment?

You can modify or reschedule your appointment at any time free of charge. Simply click the link in your confirmation SMS or call our front desk at least 4 hours before your slot.

Do I need to pay any booking fees online today?

No! Online reservation at DentaCare is 100% complimentary. All consultation and treatment fees are settled at our clinic desk after your specialist consultation.

I experience severe dental anxiety. How can you assist me?

We specialize in anxious patients. We provide computerized painless injections, noise-canceling headsets, calming music, and conscious sedation upon request.

Can I book same-day emergency appointments?

Yes! We maintain reserved emergency bays every day for acute toothaches, broken teeth, or dental trauma. Call our emergency hotline directly at +91 98765 43210.
