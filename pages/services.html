FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about our modern procedures, comfort standards, and appointment schedules.

How often should I visit the clinic for preventive dental care?

We recommend a professional ultrasonic cleaning and comprehensive oral examination every six months. This prevents tartar buildup and detects potential issues before symptoms appear.

Are root canal treatments and fillings painful?

Not at all! With computerized local anesthesia, gentle rotary endodontics, and precision apex locators, our procedures are as comfortable as receiving a routine dental filling.

Can I get same-day emergency dental relief?

Yes! We prioritize trauma, broken crowns, and severe toothaches with same-day emergency appointments. Call our direct line at +91 98765 43210 for prompt assistance.
